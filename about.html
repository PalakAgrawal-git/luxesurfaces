Our Journey of Design & Excellence

At CBWE Luxe Surfaces, we believe every surface is more than just a material. It is a canvas for timeless expression.

C Bhogilal West End (CBWE) is a 100 year old legacy company, driven by a vision to bring world class design and craftsmanship into architectural spaces. Today, CBWE Luxe Surfaces stands as one of India’s leading curators and distributors of luxury tiles and designer surfaces. Our meticulously handpicked collections blend global aesthetics with uncompromising quality, offering creations that elevate residential, commercial, and hospitality environments with enduring elegance.

Rooted in a century of excellence, C Bhogilal West End is built on trust, craftsmanship, and an unwavering commitment to quality. This heritage is further strengthened by Arihant Granites’ 60 years of industry expertise, together forming a foundation that seamlessly unites tradition with forward thinking innovation.

With decades of collective experience and a deep passion for design, we collaborate closely with architects, interior designers, and discerning clients to deliver not just surfaces, but powerful statements of style. From opulent Italian marble inspired finishes and intricate handcrafted mosaics to bold contemporary textures and patterns, every product in our portfolio reflects artistry, durability, and cutting edge design.

Our experience centres are thoughtfully crafted to inspire. These immersive spaces are where imagination meets materiality, and every detail invites you to envision the extraordinary.

We create them.
